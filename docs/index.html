Plain English

Every approval is explained: who can spend what, and why it may be risky.

Zero cost

Public RPCs and open scam lists. No API keys, accounts or servers.

You sign

Revokes are built as unsigned transactions. Your wallet signs; we never see keys.

Safety. SweepSafe never asks for a seed phrase or private key. The scanner is read-only until you click Revoke, and every revoke is confirmed in your own wallet. A low score is not a guarantee of safety.
